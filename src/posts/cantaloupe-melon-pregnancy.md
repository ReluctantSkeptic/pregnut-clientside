**The short answer:** Yes, melon is fine during pregnancy when it is **washed, freshly cut, and kept cold**. [CDC's pregnancy chart](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/pregnant-women.html) lists **freshly cut melon or cut melon kept refrigerated for 7 or fewer days** as safer, and **cut melon left out for more than 2 hours** (1 hour above 90°F) as riskier.

## Why is cantaloupe a concern?

Bacteria on the rind. The [FDA's produce advice for moms-to-be](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/fruits-veggies-and-juices-food-safety-moms-be) says to thoroughly rinse raw fruits and vegetables before cutting, **especially fruits that require peeling or cutting, like cantaloupe and other melons**, because bacteria can be on the outer rind or peel. CDC's [Listeria prevention page](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/prevention/index.html) lists cut melon among foods to handle carefully, and pregnant women are at higher risk for Listeria infection.

## How do you wash a cantaloupe?

FDA advises rinsing under running water, and using a small vegetable brush to remove surface dirt. Do not use soap, detergents, or bleach. Cut away damaged or bruised areas. Our [produce washing guide](/blog/wash-produce-pregnancy/) has the full steps.

## How long does cut melon last?

Per CDC: in the refrigerator for **7 days or fewer**, and out of the fridge for no more than **2 hours** (1 hour if hotter than 90°F, such as a picnic or hot car).

## Is pre-cut fruit from the store OK?

CDC's rule is about how long cut melon has been out of the fridge or in it. Keep pre-cut melon refrigerated and eat it within the time window; when you don't know how long a fruit tray sat out at a party, skip it.

## Common questions

### Does this apply to watermelon and honeydew?

FDA's advice says "cantaloupe and other melons." Food pages: [cantaloupe](/food/09181-melons-cantaloupe-raw/), [honeydew](/food/09184-melons-honeydew-raw/), [watermelon](/food/09326-watermelon-raw/), and [casaba](/food/09183-melons-casaba-raw/).

### Is melon a good pregnancy food otherwise?

It can be part of a varied diet. Compare vitamin C sources in our [vitamin C guide](/blog/vitamin-c-without-citrus-pregnancy/).

**The short answer:** Yes, cashews are fine to eat during pregnancy unless you are allergic to tree nuts. A **1-ounce** handful, about **18 cashews** in the [NIH iron food table](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-HealthProfessional/), adds iron, magnesium, and protein. There is no separate pregnancy limit for cashews; the practical question is how they fit your overall meals.

## How many cashews per day during pregnancy?

The public-health sources we use do not give a pregnancy-specific number of cashews. NIH food tables use **1 ounce** as the serving for nuts, which is a reasonable starting point for a snack. Cashews are energy-dense: PregNut's data lists **574 calories per 100 g** of [dry-roasted cashews](/food/12585-nuts-cashew-nuts-dry-roasted/), so a few handfuls add up quickly.

## What are the benefits of cashews in pregnancy?

**Magnesium.** The [NIH magnesium fact sheet](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-HealthProfessional/) lists **74 mg** in 1 ounce of dry-roasted cashews. Its pregnancy recommendation is **350 mg a day** at ages 19 to 30 and **360 mg** at ages 31 to 50. Our [magnesium foods guide](/blog/magnesium-foods-pregnancy/) compares more options.

**Iron.** NIH lists **2 mg** of iron in 1 ounce of oil-roasted cashews. The pregnancy recommendation is **27 mg a day**, and plant iron is absorbed less readily than iron from meat. See [iron-rich foods and pairings](/blog/iron-foods-pregnancy/).

**Zinc, protein, and folate.** Per 100 g, PregNut's historical USDA-based data lists **15.31 g protein**, **6 mg iron**, **5.6 mg zinc**, **69 mcg DFE folate**, and **61 mg choline** for dry-roasted cashews. NIH's zinc recommendation in pregnancy is **11 mg a day** for adults.

## Are cashews good for morning sickness?

[ACOG's morning sickness advice](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/morning-sickness-nausea-and-vomiting-of-pregnancy) suggests eating frequent bites of foods like nuts, fruits, or crackers, and lists nuts and nut butters among nonmeat protein foods. A small handful of cashews can be one of those bites. Our [morning sickness food guide](/blog/foods-for-morning-sickness-pregnancy/) has more ideas.

## Are salted cashews OK while pregnant?

Yes, the safety advice is the same. The difference is sodium, which PregNut's dataset does not track. Compare the **sodium line on the Nutrition Facts label**, and choose unsalted or lightly salted cashews if your clinician has asked you to watch sodium.

## Common questions

### Is cashew butter a good option?

Yes. ACOG lists nut butters among protein foods. PregNut has a separate [cashew butter entry](/food/12588-nuts-cashew-butter/); check the label for added sugar and salt.

### Are cashews a substitute for a prenatal vitamin?

No. A handful adds some iron and zinc, but it does not replace the folic acid and iron in a prenatal. See [how to read a prenatal vitamin label](/blog/prenatal-vitamin-label-pregnancy/).

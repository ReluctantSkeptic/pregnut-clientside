**The short answer:** You can eat hot dogs while pregnant if they are heated to **165°F or until steaming hot** right before you eat them. The [CDC lists unheated hot dogs as a riskier choice](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/pregnant-women.html) during pregnancy, and the [FDA advises](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/ready-eat-foods-food-safety-moms-be) not eating hot dogs unless they are reheated until steaming hot.

## Why are hot dogs a concern during pregnancy?

The concern is *Listeria*. The [FDA explains](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/ready-eat-foods-food-safety-moms-be) that *Listeria* can grow at refrigerator temperatures, and the [CDC notes](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/causes/index.html) that it can spread in food-processing facilities and is killed by heating. That is why the pregnancy guidance is to heat ready-to-eat meats such as hot dogs again before eating, even though they are sold ready to eat.

The same rule covers deli meat, cold cuts, and dry sausages. Our [deli meat guide](/blog/deli-meat-pregnancy/) covers sandwiches and cold cuts.

## How should I cook hot dogs when pregnant?

Any method works if the **center** gets hot:

- **Boil or simmer** until the hot dog is steaming hot all the way through.
- **Grill or pan-fry**, turning until heated through, not just browned on the outside.
- **Microwave**, then check the middle before eating.

A food thermometer gives the clearest answer: **165°F** in the center. Eat it soon after heating; a hot dog that has cooled on a party table is no longer "just heated."

## Are ballpark or restaurant hot dogs OK?

They can be. Ask whether hot dogs are grilled or heated to order, and choose one that is steaming hot when served. A hot dog that has been sitting in lukewarm water, or that is warm rather than hot, does not clearly meet the CDC's steaming-hot guidance.

## Can you eat corn dogs while pregnant?

Yes, when the hot dog inside is heated through. Frozen corn dogs should be cooked following the package directions until the center is **165°F or steaming hot**. Check the middle, not only the batter.

## Is it OK to eat hot dogs in every trimester?

The CDC's pregnancy food chart does not change by trimester. The same reheating guidance applies in the first, second, and third trimesters.

## What happens if I ate a cold hot dog while pregnant?

Pay attention to symptoms. For *Listeria*, the [CDC says pregnant women should call the doctor right away](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/signs-symptoms/index.html) if they have a fever and feel more tired and achy than usual. CDC's symptom table lists about **2 weeks** as when *Listeria* symptoms usually start, so tell your prenatal care team what you ate and when.

## Common questions

### Do I need to heat turkey or chicken hot dogs?

Yes. The CDC's guidance covers hot dogs generally, not only beef or pork.

### Is a hot dog with chili or sauerkraut different?

The toppings do not change the rule for the hot dog itself: heat it until steaming hot. Keep any cold toppings refrigerated until serving.

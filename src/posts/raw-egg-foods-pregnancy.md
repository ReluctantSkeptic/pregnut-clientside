**The short answer:** Avoid **homemade or fresh-made** foods that contain raw or lightly cooked eggs. The [FDA's advice for moms-to-be](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/dairy-and-eggs-food-safety-moms-be) lists Caesar salad dressing, Béarnaise sauce, hollandaise sauce, aioli, homemade mayonnaise, homemade ice cream, mousse, meringue, and tiramisu. FDA also notes that **commercial mayonnaise, dressings, and sauces contain pasteurized eggs** that are safe to eat.

## Why do raw eggs matter in pregnancy?

Raw eggs can carry *Salmonella*. The [FDA's egg safety page](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety) lists pregnant women among the people at greater risk for severe illness. [CDC's chart](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/pregnant-women.html) lists raw or undercooked (runny) eggs, and foods that contain them, as riskier choices.

## Can you eat lemon meringue pie while pregnant?

Meringue is on FDA's raw-egg list. A store-bought pie is often made with already-cooked or pasteurized ingredients, and FDA suggests using store-bought versions of these foods. For homemade meringue, use pasteurized eggs. [Lemon meringue pie](/food/18320-pie-lemon-meringue/) has its own food page.

## Is hollandaise sauce safe when pregnant?

Fresh-made hollandaise and Béarnaise are on FDA's avoid list because the yolks are only lightly cooked. Ask whether a restaurant uses pasteurized eggs, or make it at home with pasteurized eggs, which [FDA recommends](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety) for recipes that call for raw or undercooked eggs.

## Can I have Caesar salad dressing while pregnant?

Bottled Caesar dressing is fine; FDA says commercial dressings use pasteurized eggs. Homemade or tableside Caesar dressing made with raw yolks is the one to skip. See [Caesar dressing](/food/43015-salad-dressing-caesar-regular/).

## Is tiramisu safe during pregnancy?

Traditional tiramisu uses raw eggs in the cream, and FDA lists it by name. Choose a store-bought version or a recipe made with pasteurized eggs. Tiramisu also typically contains coffee and sometimes alcohol, which are separate questions; see our [caffeine guide](/blog/caffeine-coffee-tea-pregnancy/).

## What about mousse, homemade ice cream and aioli?

All three are on FDA's list when homemade with raw eggs. CDC lists **pasteurized eggs in foods that will not be cooked to a safe temperature, such as mousse and salad dressing** as the safer choice. Our [mayonnaise and aioli guide](/blog/mayonnaise-pregnancy/) and [ice cream guide](/blog/ice-cream-pregnancy/) go deeper.

## Common questions

### Are cooked egg dishes OK?

Yes. CDC says eggs cooked until yolks and whites are firm, and egg dishes cooked to 160°F (165°F if they contain meat or poultry), are safer. See our [eggs guide](/blog/eggs-during-pregnancy/).

### What about eggnog?

FDA lists eggnog that is not thoroughly cooked. See [eggnog](/food/01057-eggnog/).

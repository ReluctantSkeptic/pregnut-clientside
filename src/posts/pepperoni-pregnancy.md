**The short answer:** Pepperoni is fine during pregnancy when it is heated to **165°F or until steaming hot**, as on a pizza served hot from the oven. The [CDC lists unheated fermented or dry sausages](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/pregnant-women.html), the category pepperoni falls into, as a riskier choice, and heated ones as the safer choice.

## Can you eat pepperoni pizza while pregnant?

Yes, when the pizza is served **steaming hot**. Pepperoni baked on top of a pizza is heated with it; choose a pizza that comes out of the oven hot rather than one that has been sitting at room temperature. Toppings added cold after baking, such as [raw sprouts](/blog/sprouts-during-pregnancy/) or crumbled [queso fresco](/blog/queso-fresco-pregnancy/), have not been heated with the pizza.

## Is cold pepperoni or leftover pizza OK?

Cold pepperoni straight from the package is in the CDC's riskier column. The [CDC explains](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/causes/index.html) that *Listeria* can grow in food kept in the refrigerator and is killed by heating. Reheat leftover pepperoni pizza until it is **steaming hot** throughout, and follow our [leftovers guide](/blog/leftovers-pregnancy/) for how long to keep it.

## Is turkey or uncured pepperoni different?

No. The CDC's guidance is about **fermented or dry sausages** in general, so turkey pepperoni and products labeled "uncured" follow the same heat-it-first rule. Those label terms do not tell you the product has been heated before you eat it.

## Can you eat pepperoni sticks or charcuterie while pregnant?

Snack sticks, a cold charcuterie board, and salami slices are eaten unheated, which puts them in the CDC's riskier column. If you want them, heat them until steaming hot, for example in a cooked dish. Our [deli meat guide](/blog/deli-meat-pregnancy/) covers salami, cold cuts, and sandwiches.

## What nutrients does pepperoni provide?

Mostly protein, fat, and calories. PregNut's historical USDA-based data lists [sliced pepperoni](/food/07057-pepperoni-beef-and-pork-sliced/) at **19.25 g protein** and **504 calories** per 100 g, and [pepperoni pizza](/food/21302-pizza-pepperoni-topping-regular-crust/) at **11.74 g protein** and **153 mg calcium** per 100 g.

## Common questions

### What if I ate cold pepperoni while pregnant?

Tell your prenatal care team what you ate and when. For *Listeria*, the [CDC says pregnant women should call the doctor right away](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/signs-symptoms/index.html) if they have a fever and feel more tired and achy than usual.

### Does microwaving pepperoni count?

Yes, if it gets steaming hot. Check that the slices are hot, not just warm, before eating.

**The short answer:** Store-bought mayonnaise is fine during pregnancy. The [FDA says](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/dairy-and-eggs-food-safety-moms-be) commercial mayonnaise, dressings, and sauces contain **pasteurized eggs** that are safe to eat. The ones to avoid are **homemade or fresh-made** mayonnaise, aioli, and similar sauces made with raw eggs.

## Is store-bought mayonnaise safe during pregnancy?

Yes. The FDA's statement covers **commercial** mayonnaise as a category, so a jar from the grocery store fits it whatever the brand. The [CDC chart](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/pregnant-women.html) likewise names pasteurized eggs as the safer choice in foods that will not be cooked, such as salad dressing. Keep opened jars refrigerated as the label directs.

## Why is homemade mayonnaise different?

The FDA lists **homemade mayonnaise, aioli, Caesar dressing, hollandaise, and béarnaise** among homemade or fresh-made sauces that can contain raw eggs, and advises avoiding them during pregnancy. The CDC lists raw or undercooked eggs, and foods made with them, as a riskier choice. If you make mayo at home, the CDC names **pasteurized eggs** as the safer choice for foods that will not be cooked. Our [eggs guide](/blog/eggs-during-pregnancy/) explains how pasteurized eggs work.

## Is restaurant mayo or aioli OK while pregnant?

Ask whether the mayonnaise or aioli is **made in-house** and, if so, whether it uses pasteurized eggs. Packaged mayo fits FDA's commercial statement. A house-made garlic aioli or a fresh Caesar dressing may not, so ask for a different sauce or leave it off if the staff are not sure.

## Are tuna salad, chicken salad, and potato salad OK?

This is a separate question from the mayonnaise itself. The CDC lists **premade deli salads**, such as coleslaw and potato, tuna, chicken, or egg salad, as riskier choices during pregnancy, and **homemade** deli salads as the safer choice. Making tuna or egg salad at home with store-bought mayonnaise fits the safer column. Our [deli meat guide](/blog/deli-meat-pregnancy/) covers sandwich counters.

## What happens if I ate homemade mayo while pregnant?

Tell your prenatal care team if you are worried. The [CDC's symptom guide](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/signs-symptoms/index.html) lists *Salmonella*, which is linked to raw eggs, as starting **6 hours to 6 days** after eating, and says to see a doctor if you are pregnant and have a fever with other flu-like symptoms.

## Common questions

### Does mayonnaise have any nutrients that matter in pregnancy?

Not many per spoonful. PregNut's historical USDA-based data lists [regular mayonnaise](/food/04025-salad-dressing-mayonnaise-regular/) at **680 calories** and **34.2 mg choline** per 100 g, so it is mainly a source of fat and calories.

### Is ranch or Caesar dressing from a bottle OK?

Yes. Bottled dressings fall under FDA's statement about commercial dressings made with pasteurized eggs. Fresh-made Caesar dressing at a restaurant is the one to ask about.

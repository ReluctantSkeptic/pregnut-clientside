Behind the guides

Who writes them, and what the numbers mean

Yuriy Stasyuk publishes these guides using linked public-health and clinical sources. Food comparisons use PregNut's historical USDA-based data; they cannot establish whether a food is safe during pregnancy or what one person should eat. Each guide shows its sources and publication date, and a medical review is credited only when one has occurred. Read the editorial approach and author background.

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

PregNut is a public pregnancy nutrition information site operated by Yuriy Stasyuk. You can read its pages and search foods without creating an account.

Information you enter

The food search runs in your browser using the site's food list. A search submitted through the page may place the query in the page URL. PregNut has no contact, purchase, or account form on this site.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is optional. Its script loads only after you choose Accept analytics in the cookie banner. Google Analytics can use a first-party _ga cookie and collect usage information such as page visits, approximate location, and browser or device details. If you reject optional cookies, the site does not load the Google Analytics script. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so you do not have to answer on every page. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change it. If you reject after previously accepting, reloading stops future analytics requests; it cannot undo data already sent.

Hosting and other services

Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site and may process standard request information, such as IP address and requested pages, to provide and secure that service. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts when your browser loads the site. If you follow a link to an outside source, that site's own privacy practices apply. See Google's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy for their practices.

Questions

For questions about this policy or your data, email ystasyuk@gmail.com.

        Good to know

        Where do the targets come from?

        Each ring fills toward the pregnancy reference amount the rest of PregNut uses, mostly from the NIH pregnancy intake table for ages 19–50. See the full table. Your clinician may set different goals for you.

        How are cups and slices turned into grams?

        Each food has everyday portions based on USDA household measures, like 1 large egg (50 g) or 1 cup of cooked lentils (172 g). The nutrients come from PregNut's USDA-based food data. Brands and recipes vary, so treat the result as a close estimate.

        Is my food list saved?

        Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us, and there's no account.

        Can I get everything from food alone?

        Many people can't, especially folate, iron, DHA and vitamin D. That's why prenatal vitamins exist, and why the planned days are a starting point rather than a prescription. Ask your prenatal care team what's right for you.
